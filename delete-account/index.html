How it works

  1. Send an email with the subject Focus Cat account deletion. Include your Focus Cat user ID if you know it, and whether you used Guest or Google sign-in.
  2. If you still have the app, open Settings → Account & Privacy → Request deletion in a version that has this menu. The email draft includes a deletion request code to help verify that you control the account.
  3. We verify ownership before deleting anything. If you cannot access the app, email us anyway; we will help you verify the account. A user ID alone is not enough to authorize deletion.
  4. After verification, we remove the account and associated data and email you when it is complete. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.
Opening an email draft does not submit a request. You must send the email. No email app installed? Copy the address above into your usual email service. Never send your password, Google verification code, or login token.

What gets deleted

Your account and linked sign-in details, focus history, cat progress, coins, inventory, room layout, in-app item purchase records, associated analytics events, and account support records. Existing login sessions are invalidated. This cannot be undone.

Backups and limited retention

Deleted account data is removed from the active service. Residual copies in restricted backups are erased or sanitized within 30 days of verified deletion, and deletion is reapplied before any backup is restored to service. If a specific record must be retained for a legal obligation or a security dispute, we explain the reason and retention period when responding to your request.

After deletion is confirmed, clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove copies on your device. Signing in again can create a new, empty account.
